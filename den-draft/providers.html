Providers & models

A provider holds the credential. A model lives under a provider. A bot is built from a model.

Cost class, then status — because "free" is two different things

The most common reason a bot stays silent is not the model — it is a provider whose credential is missing, expired, or rejected. Every provider below shows its cost class and its status. A free route is free of money and not free of anything else: free pools are rate-limited and can be busy, local models are slower, an auto-router answers with a different model each time, and free tiers are often where training-on-your-inputs lives. So availability matters more than credentials for a free route — "is the box up" is the failure mode. (In my own logs this week: one parked service failing on an invalid token, one integration failing key validation, and one provider with no key at all. All three looked like "the bot is broken".)

Add a provider

Adding a provider is a documented path in the agent stack, not something this page invents. The page's job is to see what is known and what is broken — so this form hands off rather than collecting secrets in a browser.

Entered through the host's masked-entry flow, never in a page. A credential typed into a web form is a credential in a log somewhere.
