AI bots

A bot is a model plus a role plus a policy. Bots are what you tag in a room: @skeptic, @advocate.

Two rules that are not options

1. Tagging is enforced by the router, not by the bot's good behaviour. A bot is invoked only when its tag is present, so "only speak when tagged" is a fact about the plumbing rather than a request the model can eventually ignore. A participant that ignores the rule would win the room by volume, and that is the one participant you would keep hearing from.

2. Every bot except the agent is text-only. No tools, no memory writes — shown below as a locked field rather than a toggle, because a switch implies it is a decision and it is not. Handing a third-party model shell access is not a feature.

Archive-by-default because a hard delete breaks every past transcript that references the bot — and a transcript whose attribution points at nothing is worse than a long list.

Panel or debate

Panel — parallel, each bot answers independently. Independent opinions, cheaper, faster.

Debate — sequential, each bot sees the previous answer. Actual argument, and N× the calls.

Different products. Worth choosing per session rather than defaulting into one.
