A credit monitor that refuses to report good news it cannot verify.

01The problem

Provider credit had no floor check, so a silent balance drain would only be noticed when something stopped working.

The interesting failure is inside the monitor itself: a balance that cannot be read must never be reported as healthy, or the monitor is worse than having none.

02What I built

An append-only ledger tool with a three-state contract. unknown is treated as a failure (exit 1), a rejected credential is reported as rejected rather than as a zero balance, and the burn-rate projection degrades to unknown instead of crashing on missing data.

Scheduled every three days with a quiet mode, so silence means confirmed-fine rather than did-not-check.
