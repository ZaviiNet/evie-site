01The problem

A relay wired across a real fish feeder's manual button makes one feed equal one electrical pulse. The software around it lies easily: a pulse with no drum advance still charges a slot, a stale plan written yesterday still counts as due today, and a software button press can toggle the relay directly.

Any one of those over- or under-feeds a living tank, and the tank cannot complain.

02What I built

An MCP addon with a slot ledger (atomic writes, 200-entry rolling history), 15 feeder tools, and a /fish web console.

The guards are the point: paused and empty-hopper states block feeding; a failed pulse never charges a slot; the relay is always released in a finally block; and a planned target is never due unless its date matches today. Feeding is windowed (08:00-12:00 and 20:00-22:00 Europe/London) and the exact moment inside the window is chosen day by day rather than by a cron clock.
